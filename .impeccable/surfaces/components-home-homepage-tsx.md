# Home (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: shoppers in Nigeria who want foreign subscriptions without a dollar card. Action: search or browse the shop. Proof: the shop itself, shown in a 3D phone mockup. Constraints: keep SiteHeader navigation and the Browse by category section; at most three sections after the hero; light default; no gradients, glows, eyebrows; violet only for primary/active. Pinned by the brief: the Abatable hero typography and ruled grid. The phone is the owner's shots.so mockup, upscaled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product is its own hero: the real shop running in a phone on a ruled grid. Refuses the centred headline over a search pill and a card row.
OWN-WORLD: Light paper ground, 1px rules meeting at an ink cross, Geist 500 display at -0.04em, Geist text, violet only on primary buttons and active states; brand tiles in each service's own colour.
STORY: Visitor sees the shop working, reads "Every subscription you use, in one place.", recognises their apps in the marquee, picks a category, reads how buying works, and ends on the violet call to action to browse the shop.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Ruled grid, 2fr/1fr, sized to the viewport. Top left: empty. Bottom left: two-line headline at up to 96px. Right column, spanning both rows: the 3D phone mockup, wider than its column so it leans over the vertical rule, cropped at the fold. No hero search, lede, buttons or rotating line (owner's call); the header carries search and browse.
FORM: Pinned by the user (Abatable hero); no concept-seed roll. Code-led, no image generation.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
